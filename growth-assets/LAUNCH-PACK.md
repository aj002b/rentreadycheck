### Unequal roommate incomes

There are several reasonable ways to split rent, depending on what everyone agrees. For an example of $1,800 rent and annual incomes of $36,000 and $54,000, equal shares are $900 each. Splitting in proportion to income gives 40% and 60%, or $720 and $1,080. A larger room, private bathroom, and shared bills might change your agreement. Write down the method and how you'll revisit it if circumstances change.

Disclosure: I built RentReadyCheck. Its free calculator compares those options if useful: https://rentreadycheck.com/rent-split-calculator/?utm_source=community&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=rent_split_reply_01

### How much cash to save before moving

Start with the actual amounts due before you get the keys. For a hypothetical $1,200 apartment, first rent plus a $1,200 deposit is $2,400. If moving, utility setup, and household basics add $850, the upfront subtotal is $3,250. Choosing to retain a $1,000 buffer brings the planning total to $4,250. Your listing may also have fees or last-month rent; ask for a written list and payment dates, then use your own numbers.

Disclosure: I built RentReadyCheck, which has a free itemized tool: https://rentreadycheck.com/move-in-cost-calculator/?utm_source=community&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=move_in_reply_01

### $1,500 rent and income requirements

If the listing says gross monthly income must be three times rent, $1,500 rent corresponds to $4,500 gross a month, or $54,000 a year. A 2.5x requirement would be $3,750 a month, or $45,000 a year. Those are screening examples; a budget needs a separate check of take-home pay, utilities, debts, and other expenses. Ask the property manager which requirement and income sources they actually use.

Disclosure: I built RentReadyCheck. This page shows the formulas and an editable example: https://rentreadycheck.com/how-much-income-to-rent-1500/?utm_source=community&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=income_reply_01
